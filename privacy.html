Privacy Policy — OneBill PH

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · This policy lives at onebillph.com/privacy.html

OneBill PH helps you keep track of the bills that arrive in your email, and reminds you before they are due. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and where it goes.

The short version. Your email is read on your phone. We do not operate any server, we have no copy of your data, and nothing you see in the app is uploaded anywhere. There are no ads. We never take payments and never move your money. Uninstall the app and everything it knows is gone.

Who we are

OneBill PH is developed and published by OneBillph, Philippines. Website: onebillph.com. Contact: onebillph@gmail.com.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Gmail (read-only) — with your permission, via Google sign-in. Used only to find bill emails and read the amount, due date and account number. The app never sends, deletes or changes any email.
  • Yahoo Mail (read-only) — using an app password you generate yourself in your Yahoo account. This is not your Yahoo password, and you can revoke it at any time from Yahoo → Security → App passwords.
  • Attachments — PDF and image statements are opened on your phone so the amount can be read. Passwords you provide for protected PDF statements are used only to open those files.
  • Camera (optional) — only when you choose "Snap a paper bill". The photo is read on the phone and not kept afterwards.
  • Notifications — to remind you 3 days before, and on, a due date, and to tell you when a new bill arrives.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • The bills the app found: biller, amount, due date, account or reference number, billing period, and the text it read from the email (kept so a wrong amount can be diagnosed).
  • Your Google sign-in token, so you don't have to sign in every time.
  • Your Yahoo app password and any statement (PDF) passwords you save — these are held in Android's encrypted storage.
  • Payments you mark as done, and any bills you add by hand.

Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app (allowBackup="false"), so this data is not copied to your Google Drive backup either.

How we protect your data

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all Google user data and other sensitive data the app handles. Specifically:

  • Encryption in transit. Every connection the app makes is encrypted. Gmail is accessed over HTTPS/TLS using the official Google Gmail API; Yahoo Mail is accessed over IMAP with SSL/TLS on port 993. The app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • Encryption at rest. Your Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, your Yahoo app password, and any PDF statement passwords you save are stored using Android's encrypted storage (Jetpack Security EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256-GCM), with the encryption keys held in the Android Keystore and, on supported devices, protected by the phone's hardware-backed security module. Keys never leave the device and are not extractable by the app or by us.
  • Device sandbox isolation. All bill data is written to the app's private internal storage, which Android isolates from every other app on the phone. It is further protected by the device's own screen lock, PIN, pattern or biometric.
  • No server, therefore no server-side exposure. OneBill PH operates no backend, no database and no cloud storage. Because Google user data is never transmitted off your device, there is no server that could be breached, no administrator account that could be compromised, and no copy of your email anywhere outside your own phone.
  • No cloud backup. Android's automatic backup is disabled for this app (allowBackup="false"), so this data is never copied to Google Drive or any other backup service.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly) — the narrowest permission that allows a bill to be found and read. It is technically incapable of sending, modifying, labelling or deleting any message.
  • No human access. No employee, contractor or third party can read your email or bill data, because that data never reaches any system we control.
  • No AI/ML training. Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train any generalised or non-personalised artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

Data retention and deletion

  • Bill data is retained on your device only for as long as you keep the app installed, so that the dashboard and reminders keep working.
  • Disconnecting an inbox (Profile → Connected inboxes → Remove) immediately deletes the stored credentials for that inbox and revokes the app's stored token for it.
  • Deleting a bill from the dashboard removes it and the scanned text kept with it.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently erases all stored data — bills, tokens, and saved passwords. Nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was ever stored elsewhere.
  • You can additionally revoke the app's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This takes effect immediately and independently of the app.
  • To ask a question about your data, or to request confirmation of deletion, email onebillph@gmail.com.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server, and you do not create an account with us.
  • We do not upload, collect, receive or store your email, your bills, or your passwords.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone. There are no advertisers.
  • We do not track your behaviour in the app.
  • We do not process payments. When you choose to pay, the app simply opens GCash, Maya or your bank app — you complete the payment there, as you normally would.

Who else is involved

The app talks directly to the mail providers you connect — Google (Gmail API) and Yahoo (IMAP) — using your own credentials. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. No other third party receives anything.

Your control

  • Disconnect an inbox: Profile → Connected inboxes → Remove.
  • Revoke Google access entirely: myaccount.google.com/permissions → OneBill PH → Remove access.
  • Revoke Yahoo access: Yahoo → Account security → App passwords → delete the OneBill password.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app. All stored data is removed with it.

Limited Use disclosure

OneBill PH's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely to provide the bill-tracking features described above, is processed on the user's device, is not transferred to any server, is not used for advertising, and is not sold or shared. No human reads your email data.

Children

OneBill PH is intended for adults managing household bills and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and the new version will appear at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Questions or complaints

Email onebillph@gmail.com. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may also raise concerns with the National Privacy Commission.
