OneBill PH

Bill tracking that never touches your money.

Your bills already arrive in your email. OneBill PH reads them there, puts them in one list with the amount and the due date, and reminds you before each one is due — so nothing gets missed under a pile of unread mail.

Nothing leaves your phone. There is no server, no account to create, and no copy of your data anywhere but your own device. There are no ads, and the app never moves your money.

What it does

  • Finds bill emails in your Gmail or Yahoo inbox and reads the biller, amount, due date and reference number.
  • Opens PDF and image statements — including password-protected ones — to pull out the amount.
  • Reminds you three days before a due date, and again on the day.
  • Lets you snap a photo of a paper bill, or add one by hand.
  • Tracks what you have already paid.
  • When you are ready to pay, it opens GCash, Maya or your bank app — you pay there, the way you always have.

How your inboxes are connected

  • Gmail — read-only access through Google sign-in. The app never sends, deletes or changes an email.
  • Yahoo Mail — read-only, using an app password you generate yourself and can revoke at any time.

You can disconnect an inbox from inside the app, or revoke access from your Google or Yahoo account settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it knows.
